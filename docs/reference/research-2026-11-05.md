# Research sweep, 5 November 2026 (wiki, speedrunners, TAS, bots, maps, long trips, speed-ups)

Written by a Sonnet research sub-agent for Julien's request (S32). Everything here is a **lead**, to be checked against the 26.2 jar before it becomes law. Source keys in brackets are listed with their URLs at the end. "H:" marks a hypothesis of mine, not a sourced fact.

**Confidence:** H = the source states it plainly and it fits our measured numbers; M = one source, plausible; L = unclear, contradictory, or from a page I could only partly read.

**Limits of this sweep.** Most wiki pages are long and my fetcher read only the first 100,000 characters of each (history sections were often cut off). Not read or only skimmed: sweet berries, vines, lily pads, mud, bamboo, farmland, campfire, lava numbers, horses' jump, striders, Swift Sneak and Soul Speed numbers, effect percentages, wiki history tables. Speedrunner technique sources were thin (the wiki's speedrunning page is about the dragon fight, search results were SEO pages). Parkour-map licences were never stated by any listing. I did not read Mojang's code. The wiki shows 26.2 content (snapshots up to 6) and also 26.3 notes; Parkour Calculator lists 26.3 as "latest release", while the pearl page calls 26.3 "unreleased". Check which build the lab really runs; two items below are 26.3-only.

**Integrator's check against the 26.2 jar (5 November).**
- **The lab runs 26.2** (`version.json`). The 26.3 items do not apply.
- **Present in 26.2:**
  - wind charge;
  - the `wind_burst` and `lunge` enchantments;
  - the spear;
  - nautilus;
  - happy ghast;
  - sulfur caves and the potent-sulfur geyser.
- **Surprise 4 is already covered:**
  - The Rust law on `wt/law` ports `FRICTION_MODIFIER`, `AIR_DRAG_MODIFIER` and `BOUNCINESS`, as `LivingEntity` reads them.
  - The recorder writes all three into each fixture's `player` line.
- **Surprise 5 is settled, and the news is worse:**
  - `Minecraft.getTickTargetMillis` returns `max(50 ms, the server's ms per tick)`, so the client follows a slower server but never a faster one.
  - `/tick rate` above 20 doesn't speed up the bot. Job 1.8 addresses this.

## 5. Long-distance tests (R-far)

What breaks pathfinders over 1,000-10,000 blocks, from the issues above: unplanned water at the end (#24); Y overweighting (#62); fresh chunk hazards (#256); lag and unloaded chunks (#1022); replanning mid-fall (#102); timeouts at segment ends; lava appearing after generation.

**Terrain features to include:** deep ocean and rivers; ravines and canyons (lava below the cave line); jagged peaks and powder-snow groves; swamps (mud, lily pads, mangrove roots); villages (1.5-high fences and walls, doors); mineshafts (rails, cobweb, fences); lush caves (clay, moss, azalea, dripleaf); dripstone caves; deep dark (sculk shrieker is 8 px high, warden); ancient cities; ice spikes and frozen oceans; badlands; jungle (vines, leaves); bamboo (centre 3 px, leaves 6-10 px); trial chambers (copper doors, breeze; spawn per 34x34 chunk region); Nether biomes (soul sand valley slow; basalt, lava lakes, roof); world border and coordinates near it; unpregenerated frontier chunks (the observer sees only loaded chunks).

## 7. Surprises (most important first)

1. **The toolkit is much larger than representation.md lists.** Wind charges (a launch about 6 blocks every 10 ticks with a fall reset), the mace and Wind Burst, Lunge (27.5 m/s per level-III jab, in air), camel/nautilus dashes, riptide in rain, beds (75%), and 26.2's sulfur geysers are all absent from the cone's speed list (claim 9) and from R6/R8. If the cone's LB ignores them, a bot using them will "beat the lower bound" and the verdict will read as a bug. This is also the best source of litmus-test surprises.
2. **Wind-charge self-launch breaks the world rule in subtle ways** (claim 6): the burst flips doors, trapdoors, gates and levers, presses buttons, rings bells and shatters decorated pots (irreversible). It needs the restoration check per burst, not just per block we place.
3. **The Nether has a water-free fall clutch.** Powder snow resets fall damage in the Nether and a bucket places and takes it back; R6 treats the Nether as "no water" and so stops there.
4. **26.2 changed the law's inputs.** New entity attributes `air_drag_modifier`, `friction_modifier`, `bounciness`; bed bounce 66% to 75%; falling stalactite damage raised in snapshot 6; sulfur geysers. The Rust port (job 1.5) and the L0 dump should read whether the player carries these attributes. Cobweb-in-water and pearl-vs-border changes are 26.3 only [J262, EP, CW].
5. **`/tick freeze` does not freeze the player.** And nothing found says whether the client runs faster under `/tick rate`/`sprint`. The feedback-loop rung (S32) depends on this; measure it first, before any tick-rate gate.
6. **Wiki speed numbers conflict with our measurements and with each other.** Ice tunnel: wiki 16 m/s (2-high) and 18.5 (trapdoor ceiling) vs ours 19.3 under a ceiling and 9.2 on open ice. Riptide III: 375 m/s on Transportation, 125 on Trident (with elytra). Honey-block breaking time, flying speed 10.79 vs 10.89. Never take wiki numbers as bounds; derive LB from the law.
7. **Parkour resources are 1.8-based.** mcpk formulas are marked not exact (first 4-6 decimals), last edited 2021 [PKF]; maps assume 1.8 physics. Any jump from them must be re-solved on 26.2 (Parkour Calculator supports 26.x).
8. **Chunk loading is a design input.** Pearls and portals carry loading tickets (a pearl loads about 5x5 chunks and refreshes about every 39 ticks); everything freezes at the inactive edge; random ticks reach all loaded chunks (1.21.5+). A planner that treats the observer's world as full truth fails at the frontier (Baritone #256, #1022).
9. **The boat fall rule is still ambiguous on the wiki** (negates vs "does not reduce in water deeper than 1"). It stays open; resolve it in the jar.
10. **Damage boost and some other techniques rely on client/server velocity desync** that mcpk itself calls not fully understood. Under a vanilla server they may be corrected as "lagback"; the ladder counts corrections as failures, so these must be classified before a rung is frozen.
11. **Nothing found contradicts the 80-tick portal wait, the 8:1 scaling, sprint cutoff at food 6, or the 0.003 threshold** (covered; sources agree). The portal search radii (128 Overworld, 16 Nether) and the forced-creation fallback (a 2x3 obsidian platform overwriting blocks) are new to the docs and sharpen claim 12.
12. **No standard A-to-B pathfinding benchmark exists in public** (Voyager/LoopNav/MineAnyBuild test planning or video models). R-far and the trip table above would be the first.
