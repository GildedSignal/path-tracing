## Roadmap (approved 9 November, S48; reordered 10 November, S65, and 13 November, S95)

Every phase ends in a **flight**, something Julien uses in the game, and has a **kill criterion** that re-derives the representation instead of patching it. After Phase 3 the scoreboard reorders the rest, largest gap first.

0. **One line of truth.** Done.
1. **The ground.** Done except L0 and job 1.5. Flight: the bot's runs on his tracks replayed offline at ≥ 10⁴× real time; his T is the bar (S56). Kill: no path below about 5 µs means the `World` interface is wrong.
2. **The first flight** (slice A; first after the ground since 13 November, S95: one bot flies through the lab client and needs no server mod). The record is version 2, in the code (3.2a, 3.2c). The planner (3.2b, building): A* over exact states with words as edges, pruning only by proof, the table looked up by class alone, backward lookup by mirrored reach tables if the lanes confirm it, the slack allocator. The executor: re-anchors at every event, prices veto risk (S62). **Flight: one bot races his 18 tracks; count the jumps where no sentence arrives or his T beats ours; his runs replayed through the law are the diagnostic.** Kill: words per regime ≫ 10³; no failure the loop can use.
3. **Learning where the flight fails** ([learning.md](learning.md)): hand-made maps from simple to the hardest, the port following what the maps need; one bot. Flight: the bot clears a map he picks that it could not clear before. Kill: a solved jump that does not transfer to jumps of the same shape; cost per new puzzle not falling ([vocabulary.md](vocabulary.md) section 11).
4. **Slice B, one tool, and words that edit the world (S65, S95).** Forward owns history (the edited world, the inventory); backward sets carry premises (cells at ticks, what must be in hand). R-lego: blocks and one gap past the sprint-jump limit ([ladder.md](ladder.md)). It needs the placement law (pinned in the wiki), operator segments with `writes`, `needs` and obligations, the law read backwards, the archive by effect, the obligation ledger and the discoveries log ([scales.md](scales.md) 4.1, 4.9). Kill: ≫ 10³ verifications per gap, a tool needing search code, or a found word that isn't reused.
5. **Slice C, one mind in the server, five bots sharing one tool (S55, S65).** [server.md](server.md): bots that don't observe, world changes streamed once, the vanilla client's packets, a self channel, lazy bots on quiet footprints, the engine as a separate process; bots are players (S52); the deviation stream and a sandbox server at 100×. Plus one reservation; another bot's tube is a fact in four dimensions. Flight also: 10–50 bots as players (S53). Rungs: a shared crossing; a rolling bridge cleaned up by the last bot. Flight: he watches five bots cooperate.
6. **Then scale, by the scoreboard:** the medium and far field (R-scale, R-far), tube tickets and lazy bots, more tools (R6, R7, R8, R-brach, R10, the Baritone benchmark), the swarm at 10³ and 10⁴, the economy and building ((max, +), reverse demolition, prices as fields), then the cherry (learned ordering, the LLM overseer, the GPU where profiling points, the human-limits profile, and his easter eggs, S66: idle bots near someone typing look at them, sneak and nod; when a bot falls to its death the others mock it).
