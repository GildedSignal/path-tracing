## 1. The Causal Chain from Plate/Button Cells to Crouch-Walking

   - Instead, button (`508`) and plate (`510`) models enter in `Planner::arc` ([search.rs](file:///D:/path-tracing-wt/play/engine/plan/src/search.rs#L270-L332)) via `arith::free_tick` ([arith.rs](file:///D:/path-tracing-wt/play/engine/plan/src/arith.rs#L32-L55)) and in `Planner::gait` ([search.rs](file:///D:/path-tracing-wt/play/engine/plan/src/search.rs#L281)) via `Cycle::layered` ([arith.rs](file:///D:/path-tracing-wt/play/engine/plan/src/arith.rs#L90-L106)).

Both `Lane::Arc` and `Lane::Gait` shut off.
2. **Why `Lane::Replay` enters and stays in crouch-walk (`forward, sneak`, sprint off, jump off):**
   - **Entering sneak:** When `Lane::Arc` stops around tick 20 and search falls back to `run_segment` ([segment.rs](file:///D:/path-tracing-wt/play/engine/words/src/segment.rs#L88-L114)), on a 1-tick segment cut by an event that fires regardless of `sneak` (e.g., `no_jump_delay` hitting `1 -> 0` at [segment.rs](file:///D:/path-tracing-wt/play/engine/words/src/segment.rs#L78) or landing `on_ground` at [segment.rs](file:///D:/path-tracing-wt/play/engine/words/src/segment.rs#L74)), [movement.rs](file:///D:/path-tracing-wt/play/engine/law/src/movement.rs#L150-L160) updates `state.crouching` from the *previous* tick's `shift_key_down` (`false`) *before* writing `state.shift_key_down = input.sneak` ([movement.rs](file:///D:/path-tracing-wt/play/engine/law/src/movement.rs#L152)). Thus `slowly` ([movement.rs](file:///D:/path-tracing-wt/play/engine/law/src/movement.rs#L160)) is `false` on tick 1 even for `input.sneak = true`, giving the `sneak = true` and `sneak = false` children identical `position`, `velocity`, `g`, and `h`. Because `tiny_programs()` ([program.rs](file:///D:/path-tracing-wt/play/engine/words/src/program.rs#L137-L143)) generates `sneak` predicates `Always`/`OnGround` *after* `Not(Always)`, the `sneak = true` child gets a larger `node` id, and `Entry::cmp` ([search.rs](file:///D:/path-tracing-wt/play/engine/plan/src/search.rs#L91-L94)) tie-breaks equal keys by `self.node.cmp(&other.node)` (*"among equals the node made last"*), popping `shift_key_down = true` first. On the next expansion, [movement.rs](file:///D:/path-tracing-wt/play/engine/law/src/movement.rs#L150) flips `crouching: false -> true` (`slowly = true`) on tick 1 for *all* programs, cutting every non-jump program at `ticks = 1` ([segment.rs](file:///D:/path-tracing-wt/play/engine/words/src/segment.rs#L76)) with equal `h`, and the same last-node tie-breaker pops `shift_key_down = true, pose = Crouching, crouching = true`.
   - **Staying in crouch-walk:** Once grounded with `shift_key_down = true, pose = Crouching, crouching = true`, every non-sneak program (`input.sneak = false`) flips `shift_key_down` and `pose` on tick 1 ([movement.rs](file:///D:/path-tracing-wt/play/engine/law/src/movement.rs#L152), [movement.rs](file:///D:/path-tracing-wt/play/engine/law/src/movement.rs#L532)) and every jump program flips `on_ground` on tick 1, so `events()` ([segment.rs](file:///D:/path-tracing-wt/play/engine/words/src/segment.rs#L74-L76)) cuts all of them at `ticks = 1` (~0.06 blocks west, with `Lane::Arc` blocked). Only crouch-walk (`sneak = true, jump = false`) flips no state flag, running ~15 ticks until `BodyBlock`/`main_supporting_block` crosses into the next block ([segment.rs](file:///D:/path-tracing-wt/play/engine/words/src/segment.rs#L76-L81), 1.0 block west). Because `mode = '5'` ([flight.rs](file:///D:/path-tracing-wt/play/engine/plan/src/flight.rs#L136)) uses greedy `Order::Field` (`improving = false`, `key = [node.h, -node.g]` at [search.rs](file:///D:/path-tracing-wt/play/engine/plan/src/search.rs#L160)) until the first sentence ([search.rs](file:///D:/path-tracing-wt/play/engine/plan/src/search.rs#L492)), `node.g` is excluded from `key[0]`: the 15-tick, 1-block crouch-walk child has a ~3.5 lower `h` ([reach.rs](file:///D:/path-tracing-wt/play/engine/plan/src/reach.rs#L309-L326)) than any 1-tick un-crouch child and wins at every block.
   - **Why `"sprint": false` appears:** `state.sprinting` stays `true` from ticks 0..20 whenever `input.forward` is `true` without horizontal collision ([movement.rs](file:///D:/path-tracing-wt/play/engine/law/src/movement.rs#L226-L235)). Since `for sprint in [false, true]` ([program.rs](file:///D:/path-tracing-wt/play/engine/words/src/program.rs#L140)) orders `sprint = false` first and `Planner::identity` ([search.rs](file:///D:/path-tracing-wt/play/engine/plan/src/search.rs#L170)) strips `state.input`, `Planner::push` ([search.rs](file:///D:/path-tracing-wt/play/engine/plan/src/search.rs#L182)) discards the identical `sprint = true` twin as `dominated`.
   - **Why `slide` cannot recover sprinting:** `slides()` ([polish.rs](file:///D:/path-tracing-wt/play/engine/plan/src/polish.rs#L23-L26)) and `slide()` ([polish.rs](file:///D:/path-tracing-wt/play/engine/plan/src/polish.rs#L29-L64)) only perturb each step's `ticks` (`Â±1`, or `0` when `ticks == 1`) and `yaw_index` (`Â±1 mod 64`), never `step.program`.

## 2. Whether the Class Is Wrong or the Table Is

A button (`508`) or pressure plate (`510`) cell is **provably identical to air (`378`) on every comparison `movement.rs` makes**; treating them as distinct from air in `arith::plain`, `Cycle::layered`, and `pattern::encode` is the defect:
- All three models ([models.tsv](file:///D:/path-tracing-wt/play/engine/world/data/models.tsv#L379), lines 379, 509, 511; [snapshot.rs](file:///D:/path-tracing-wt/play/engine/world/src/snapshot.rs#L69-L97)) have empty collision shapes (`f[15,17,18,19] = ""`), `inside = Inside::None` (`f[13] = "none"`, [snapshot.rs](file:///D:/path-tracing-wt/play/engine/world/src/snapshot.rs#L93)), `grounded_contact = GroundedContact::None` (`f[14] = "none"`), `stuck_multiplier = None` (`f[12] = ""`), `fluid = Fluid::EMPTY`, `friction = 0.6`, `speed_factor = 1.0`, and `jump_factor = 1.0`.
- Model `510`'s `"true"` in column 11 (`f[11]`, [models.tsv](file:///D:/path-tracing-wt/play/engine/world/data/models.tsv#L511)) is not an inside effect: [snapshot.rs](file:///D:/path-tracing-wt/play/engine/world/src/snapshot.rs#L92) parses `f[11]` into `fluid_flow: Some(FluidFlowBlock { blocks_motion: true, .. })`, which is read only in `Fluid::current` ([world.rs](file:///D:/path-tracing-wt/play/engine/law/src/world.rs#L88-L113)) when a cell contains non-empty fluid ([movement.rs](file:///D:/path-tracing-wt/play/engine/law/src/movement.rs#L887-L901)).
- Flag `4096` (`f[5]`) sets `world_hook = true, air = false` ([snapshot.rs](file:///D:/path-tracing-wt/play/engine/world/src/snapshot.rs#L74-L84)). `Block::world_hook` ([world.rs](file:///D:/path-tracing-wt/play/engine/law/src/world.rs#L213)) is **never read** in `movement.rs` or `world.rs`. `Block::air` ([world.rs](file:///D:/path-tracing-wt/play/engine/law/src/world.rs#L214)) is read only at [movement.rs](file:///D:/path-tracing-wt/play/engine/law/src/movement.rs#L1028) inside `inside_segment`, which is skipped when `!world.has_inside_effects()` ([movement.rs](file:///D:/path-tracing-wt/play/engine/law/src/movement.rs#L989); `false` here by [snapshot.rs](file:///D:/path-tracing-wt/play/engine/world/src/snapshot.rs#L156)), and even when reached, `inside_block` ([movement.rs](file:///D:/path-tracing-wt/play/engine/law/src/movement.rs#L1064)) matches `Inside::None => {}` and changes no state bit (only occupying a slot in the 16-cell per-axis `visited` buffer at [movement.rs](file:///D:/path-tracing-wt/play/engine/law/src/movement.rs#L992-L1036)).

## 3. Smallest Representation Change

1. **Drop `!block.world_hook` from `arith::plain`** ([arith.rs](file:///D:/path-tracing-wt/play/engine/plan/src/arith.rs#L20)): `world_hook` is not a comparison of `movement.rs`. Removing `&& !block.world_hook` makes `plain(block, free)` test only the law's actual flying-body comparisons, so `Lane::Arc` sprint-jumps across buttons and plates just as on `stonesimple`.
2. **Match `Cycle::layered` and `pattern::encode` to the law's reads:** in `pattern::encode` ([pattern.rs](file:///D:/path-tracing-wt/play/engine/law/src/pattern.rs#L408-L426)), omit `world_hook`, `fluid_flow` when `fluid.kind == FluidKind::Empty`, and `air` when `inside == Inside::None && stuck_multiplier.is_none()`; and in `Cycle::layered` ([arith.rs](file:///D:/path-tracing-wt/play/engine/plan/src/arith.rs#L102)), compare cells by `cell_digest` under the cycle's read mask rather than `std::ptr::eq`.
