**New defects**

- “Every test … returns” three values, but an unknown membership opens exact search without specifying its result when that search exhausts its budget. A refusal is specified; whether the planner may return another certified sentence while unresolved alternatives remain is not.
- The permitted replayed intermediate is not required to establish a useful, newly reached state under matched conditions. An irrelevant replay can satisfy a mover.
- “No track-specific input … anywhere” literally forbids the track geometry and start state needed to solve a track. It must distinguish instance data from stored answers or tuning.
- “Same … precomputation” for both methods conflicts with comparing methods that may require different artifacts. Matching the accounting and availability of precomputation is distinct from requiring identical artifacts.
- Provenance requires “the key that makes its lookup need no post-click work,” although reading a key and performing a lookup require work. The intended accounting is achievable; that literal requirement is not.
- “No number in a set is chosen by you” conflicts with representing numerical bounds unless it defines how derived bounds differ from builder-selected representation parameters.

**Buildability:** Not reliably buildable as written. The literal no-track-input and zero-work-lookup requirements are unachievable, and the finite-budget `unknown` outcome and mover witness need decisions an unattended builder would otherwise invent.

**Overspecification:** “Sets backward,” “the point … forward,” splitting only at law comparisons, and forbidding a grid or MCTS prescribe representation or mechanism beyond the measured acceptance requirements. “Never a rescan” also dictates the repair mechanism rather than solely requiring correct local invalidation and its measured cost.
