1. **The job census.** One row per job attempt: job id, the model that ran it (from the prompt, the HANDOFF text, or the `Built-by:` trailer; `unknown` if the record does not say — never guess), the **job shape** you assign it from this closed list — `tight-spec-build`, `open-ended-build`, `measurement-probe`, `judge`, `jar-read`, `doc-or-spec`, `port-delta` — the outcome from this closed list — `done`, `done-with-findings`, `incomplete`, `stopped-on-missing-input`, `judged-FAIL` — net lines changed, and the one sentence from `last.md` that says what it stalled on (quote it, do not paraphrase).
2. **The GLM census.** Lanes run, reproduced / lead / refuted counts, and the reproduced rate. Separate the leads that died on 429 from the real ones. Then the same rate split by lens family (`port-*`, `l0-*`, trials, everything else).
3. **The discriminating findings.** Not a summary: the three to six places where the record actually separates one model from another. For each, name the job ids on both sides. A difference seen once is a lead, not a result — say which it is. Where the record cannot separate two models, say so plainly; that row belongs to cost, not to skill.
4. **What the record cannot say.** Argon and Hydrogen have never run here, so they have zero rows. Name every other blind spot: job shapes with fewer than three attempts, models never given a given shape, and any outcome that was decided by the spec's quality rather than the model's.
